Pots Office portable mode

While this file sits next to artcraft-launcher.exe, Pots Office keeps its settings, its
download cache and its record of installed apps in the ArtCraftLauncherData folder beside it, and
writes nothing to %APPDATA%. Copy the whole folder to move Pots Office with its settings.

Delete this file to use the normal per-user settings folder (%APPDATA%\Pots Office) instead.
If ArtCraftLauncherData can't be written (for example on a read-only drive), Pots Office says
so and uses %APPDATA%\Pots Office instead.
